Art guide

Draw your sprites on one sheet, put it in public/textures/art/mine/ with an art.json, and the game cuts them into paper cards. This page shows how the game numbers the drawings on your sheet, and every card slot you can paint. Full steps: docs/YOUR_ART.md.

Card slots, by kind

Map a whole kind ("kinds": { "tree": [1, 2, 3] }) or one slot by name ("slots": { "trees_a_4": 2 }). Kinds marked fill are walls and roofs: your picture is stretched to the slot and the card keeps its folded shape. Every other kind is fitted (feet on the ground) and the card is re-cut to your outline.

Animals

Animals are flat standees that face right: "animals": { "fox": 9 } uses drawing 9 for every pose, or name poses one by one ({ "walk_a": 9, "walk_b": 10, "*": 9 }).
